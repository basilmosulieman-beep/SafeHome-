  <p>SafeHome is a personal finance application. This policy describes how information is handled.</p>

  <h2>Information Collection</h2>
  <p>SafeHome does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.</p>
  <p>All data entered into the app (transactions, budgets, goals, buffer amounts, and related settings) is stored exclusively on the device using local storage. No data leaves the device.</p>

  <h2>Data Not Collected</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>No accounts or authentication</li>
    <li>No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data</li>
    <li>No financial credentials</li>
    <li>No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers</li>
    <li>No data shared with third parties</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Data Storage</h2>
  <p>All information remains on the device. Deleting the app removes the data. No cloud backup or synchronization is performed.</p>

  <h2>Permissions</h2>
  <p>The app does not request device permissions such as camera, contacts, or location.</p>

  <h2>Children’s Privacy</h2>
  <p>The app is not directed at children under 13. No information is knowingly collected from children.</p>
